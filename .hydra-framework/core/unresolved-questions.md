# Unresolved High-Impact Questions

These are intentionally not solved in the seed. Resolve them one at a time when they become consequential. Implemented task-record and initial provider-adapter choices are documented in their canonical owners rather than repeated here.

1. Should Obsidian be canonical storage, a generated view, a private workspace, a shared workspace, or a mixed surface with explicit boundaries?
2. Which generated cognition structures are worth storing, and which should always be rebuilt on demand?
3. How should successful repository-specific improvements be evaluated and promoted into the common seed?
4. What exact machine-readable task-state schema is needed after graph or harness automation proves a companion useful?
