# Archive And Supersession

Hydra preserves useful history without letting old or wrong material remain authoritative.

## Archive

Archive material when it is obsolete, abandoned, replaced, or no longer useful for active retrieval.

Archived material may still be evidence. It is not current source of truth unless explicitly restored.

## Supersession

Supersede material when a newer decision, source, validation result, implementation change, or correction replaces it.

A supersession note should identify:

- old material
- new authority
- reason for replacement
- date
- affected knowledge spaces or tasks

## Previous Attempts

Previous attempts can be useful evidence, especially when they explain why a path failed.

They must be labeled clearly when they are not trusted:

- `previous-attempt`
- `superseded`
- `rejected`
- `do-not-use-as-source-of-truth`

## Rule

Do not delete architectural learning just because the artifact is stale. Preserve the lesson, move or mark the artifact, and make the current authority obvious.
