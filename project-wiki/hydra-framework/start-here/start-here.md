# Start Here

Audience: A first-time Hydra reader who needs a safe starting route

Reader goal: Identify the next page or setup action without reading framework internals

Page type: orientation

Hydra is the repository's shared AI engineering system. The [project wiki
home](/project-wiki/home.md) is the human index. This page gives a new reader
the shortest route into the part of Hydra they need.

## Answer

Choose one row below. Use [New Contributor](/project-wiki/hydra-framework/start-here/new-contributor.md)
for a fresh clone, [Working With Hydra](/project-wiki/hydra-framework/working-with-hydra/working-with-hydra.md)
for work today, and [Operations](/project-wiki/hydra-framework/operations/operations.md)
when a gate or diagnostic is the immediate need.

## Before you begin

For a normal clone, follow the [New Contributor
route](/project-wiki/hydra-framework/start-here/new-contributor.md). To bring
Hydra into a different repository, follow the [new-repository adoption
route](/project-wiki/hydra-framework/start-here/adopt-a-repository.md). The
[Hydra Framework](/project-wiki/hydra-framework/hydra-framework.md) page
explains the framework's layout, while [Working With Hydra](/project-wiki/hydra-framework/working-with-hydra/working-with-hydra.md)
is the practical daily route.

## Important boundary

This page chooses a route. It does not replace the canonical rules under
`.hydra-framework/`, and it does not establish that a named architecture layer
is an available provider runtime. For orchestration, read [Execution
Stack](/project-wiki/hydra-framework/architecture/execution-stack.md); for
current implementation status, follow [Build
Status](/.hydra-framework/repo/knowledge/spaces/hydra-framework/units/build-status.md).
